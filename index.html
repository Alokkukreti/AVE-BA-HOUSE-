"Ba."
A syllable before language. The instinctive shape of trust.

Ave-Ba began with a sound. The younger son, Aveer, calling out to his elder brother — Ba — in the shorthand that exists between people who belong to each other. It carried ease, comfort, the certainty of being held.

We pressed that feeling into objects for the home: knitted throws drawn close on a cold night, blankets that hold a child in the dark, cushions that find their place quietly on a sofa over years. Everything we make is knitted from natural fibres and built to be lived with — not for a season, but for the years in which a house becomes a home.

Made to Belong
Our promise, and how to care for your pieces
The Fibre
Knitted from natural cotton and cotton-blend yarns chosen for softness, durability and the way they age — growing more characterful, never threadbare, with the years.
The Making
Each piece is knitted and finished by hand. Small irregularities are not faults but the signature of something made by people, not machines.
The Care
Cold gentle machine wash or hand wash. Dry flat in shade. Do not tumble dry or wring. Store folded, not hung. Treated kindly, these pieces outlast seasons.
The Promise
Not just beautiful things, but the ones that belong — objects intended for daily life and long keeping, made to become part of how a home feels.
